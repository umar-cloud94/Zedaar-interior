Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How does the design process start?

With a free on-site consultation. We visit your space, understand your requirements and budget, and follow up with 3D concepts within a week.

What is the typical project timeline?

Most full-home projects are delivered in 60 days from design lock, depending on scope. Single-room projects can be quicker.

Do you handle both design and execution?

Yes — sourcing, contractors, on-site supervision and final styling are all managed in-house, with one point of contact throughout.

Can I customise materials and finishes?

Every project is fully bespoke. We'll show you material and finish options suited to your taste and budget before anything is locked in.

Which areas in Hyderabad do you serve?

We currently work across Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Gachibowli and surrounding areas — get in touch if you're elsewhere in the city.
